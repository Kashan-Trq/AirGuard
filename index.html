    TRANSPARENCY

    What AirGuard knows — and what it doesn’t

    01

    Forecast, not diagnosis

    AirGuard does not predict asthma, disease, or individual health outcomes. It supports environmental planning.

    02

    Relative, not “safe”

    The app identifies lower-exposure periods among the available hours. It never labels an outdoor period as medically safe.

    03

    Source visible

    The current public build uses CAMS air-quality forecasts via Open-Meteo and weather forecasts via Open-Meteo.

    04

    Built for validation

    The product is structured so a Lahore sensor network or independent ML forecast can replace the public forecast source later.

    RESEARCH BUILD

    From forecast to intervention

    AirGuard’s research question is not whether pollution is harmful. It asks whether short-term environmental forecasts can support scheduling choices that reduce modeled exposure during outdoor activities.
