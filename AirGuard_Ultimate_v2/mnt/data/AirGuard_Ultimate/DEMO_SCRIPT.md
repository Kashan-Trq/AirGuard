# AirGuard 90-Second Demo Script

**0–10 s — Problem**
“Most air-quality apps tell me the AQI. But when my brother wants to play football or a school has assembly, the real question is: what should we do with that information?”

**10–30 s — Ask AirGuard**
Type: `When should my younger brother play football tomorrow between 3 PM and 6 PM for 45 minutes?`

Show the answer card. Explain that AirGuard searches every feasible 15-minute start time and returns the lowest modeled PM2.5 window that still fits the real schedule.

**30–45 s — One Better Hour**
Show the daily One Better Hour. Say: “The city may not become clean today. AirGuard tries to find one decision that can still be better.”

**45–65 s — School Mode**
Show assembly, recess, and PE. Run the optimizer. Explain that it schedules several outdoor activities without overlap while minimizing the total modeled concentration-time exposure proxy.

**65–78 s — Human + impact layer**
Show Care Circle and save a plan. Explain that it becomes impact only after the user marks it completed. AirGuard records completed better-air minutes and modeled proxy difference locally on the device.

**78–90 s — Evidence**
Show Sensor Lab. “A school can upload its own PM2.5 sensor CSV locally and compare the forecast-based decision with observed pollution. AirGuard is built to be tested, not just demonstrated.”

End: **“AirGuard turns air quality from a number into a decision.”**
