DETAILED PLANNER

Give AirGuard your constraints

It ranks every 15-minute start time that fits your schedule. PM2.5 drives the ranking; rain, heat and UV are shown as practical flags, not hidden inside a mystery score.

WHY TRUST THE OUTPUT?

Transparent by design

01

Relative, never “safe”

AirGuard ranks lower-exposure options. It does not declare a polluted period medically safe.

02

No black-box score

PM2.5 determines the main ranking. Weather conditions are displayed separately as practical flags.

03

Data source visible

Public mode uses modeled air-quality forecasts through Open-Meteo and weather forecasts through Open-Meteo.

04

Sensor-ready

The built-in Sensor Lab can analyze observed PM2.5 files locally for school-pilot validation.

05

Local privacy

Care Circle and impact logs stay in browser storage. No account is required.

06

Offline resilience

The installable PWA caches the app shell and can fall back to the last stored forecast when the network fails.
